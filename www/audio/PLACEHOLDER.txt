TEMPORARY DEVELOPMENT AUDIO

These WAV files are generated locally by a script in this repository.
They are not final Shutdown sound design.
They are not commercial or third-party recordings.
Brown Noise, Rain, and Ocean are simple synthesized loops so playback can be tested.
threshold-enter.wav, threshold-exit.wav, and sweep-texture.wav are temporary Threshold and Sweep placeholders from scripts/generate-shutdown-audio.mjs.
They are reproducible from AUDIO_SEED 20260926. sweep-texture.wav is an 11-second stereo fallback: left, center, right, then darker and back to center as it dissolves.
Replace them before release.
